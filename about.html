About Prematex Roofing

Your trusted roofing partner in the Dallas Metroplex

Protecting Dallas Homes & Businesses Since Day One

At Prematex Roofing, we understand that your roof is more than just shingles and nails—it's the shield that protects your family, your business, and your most valuable investment. That's why we approach every project with the same level of care and attention to detail, whether it's a residential repair or a complete commercial roof replacement.

Based in Plano and serving the entire Dallas Metroplex, we've built our reputation on honest work, transparent pricing, and exceptional customer service. We're not just contractors; we're your neighbors, committed to keeping our community safe and dry.

Our team of experienced roofing professionals brings decades of combined expertise to every job. We stay current with the latest roofing technologies and techniques, ensuring that you receive the most advanced and effective solutions available.
